Privacy, third parties and accessibility

What this site collects

Nothing. Transmission Pending sets no cookies, has no accounts, runs no analytics and sends nothing about you to any server we control. It is a static site with no backend.

What it remembers

Your choices (room, colour, Calm mode, focus mode and whether you have seen the notice) are kept in your browser’s session storage for this visit only. They are cleared when you close the tab. A link you paste is kept in memory only and is not stored or logged.

YouTube

When you choose a channel or paste a link and press Continue, this site loads the YouTube player. This site uses YouTube API Services. The video and its sound come from YouTube and the channel’s owner, not from this site. YouTube may set cookies or collect data once the player loads. We load it from youtube-nocookie.com, which reduces this but does not remove it. By watching you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). The Google Privacy Policy is at http://www.google.com/policies/privacy. You can revoke this site’s access to your Google data, if any, at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

When your device contacts third parties

Only after you press Continue on the notice. Before that, every request goes to this site. After it:

Third-party origins contacted after Continue
Origin What for
www.youtube.com the player loader script
www.youtube-nocookie.com the player itself

Room sound

The optional room sound is made in your browser and downloads nothing. It is off until you turn it on.

Channels

The channel guide lists channels that publish live video on YouTube. Names are used only to identify the source. This site does not host, copy, record, relay or change any video. State-run channels are labelled. Not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Prasar Bharati, Doordarshan, Sansad TV or any listed channel. Doordarshan and Sansad TV channels are shown through their YouTube embeds. NASA material is credited to NASA and does not imply its endorsement.

Flashing and accessibility

We make the site to WCAG 2.2 level AA. We cannot change or check the video that YouTube plays, so third-party video is outside our conformance claim: it may be loud or contain flashing. Calm mode starts nothing by itself, freezes the rooms, and asks for captions where the player allows. You can stop any video with Stop at any time. Captions and other controls are in the player.
